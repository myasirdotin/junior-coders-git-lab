## 🎯 Course Overview

Security is not an afterthought added at the end of development; it is the foundation of digital trust. This textbook equips students to think like ethical defenders—understanding how attackers exploit human psychology, web code, and network packets, and building impenetrable defenses.

In this textbook, students will:

1. **Adopt the Defender's Mindset**: Understand the CIA Triad (Confidentiality, Integrity, Availability), Defense in Depth, and Islamic ethics of digital stewardship (*Amānah*).
2. **Master Authentication & Passwords**: Password entropy calculations, GPU cracking mathematics, salted hashing with Argon2id/bcrypt, and Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA).
3. **Deconstruct Social Engineering**: Spot phishing lures, typosquatting domains, pretexting calls, spoofed email headers (SPF/DKIM), and psychological manipulation.
4. **Defeat the OWASP Top 10**: Prevent Cross-Site Scripting (XSS), SQL Injection (SQLi) via Prepared Statements, CSRF tokens, and Insecure Direct Object References (IDOR).
5. **Differentiate Cryptographic Primitives**: Encoding vs Encryption vs Hashing, the Avalanche Effect, and public Wi-Fi protection via TLS 1.3 and VPN tunnels.
6. **Practice Daily Digital Hygiene**: Device malware defenses, ransomware recovery, 3-2-1 backups, automatic patching, and EXIF metadata stripping.
7. **Perform Incident Response**: The 6 phases of containment and recovery, digital forensics, and ethical vulnerability disclosure (*Ṣidq*).

## 🌟 Islamic Ethical Framework (*Amānah & Ṣidq*)

All lessons in this curriculum adhere to our core ethical standards:

- **Data Privacy as a Sacred Trust (*Amānah*)**: User data and sensitive communications must be guarded. Breaching or exposing private data without authorization violates this sacred trust.
- **Truthfulness (*Ṣidq*)**: Absolute prohibition of deceptive social engineering, phishing, fraud, or fraudulent impersonation.
- **Strictly Halal Contexts**: No malware creation, no unauthorized intrusion tutorials, no extortion simulators, and no surveillance tools targeting innocent individuals.
- **Craftsmanship & Diligence (*Iḥsān*)**: Build secure architectures with thorough validation, defensive parameterization, and robust error handling.
