Contact Us

Found a mistake, or have an idea for the site? Here is how to tell us.

Students

Tell your teacher what you found: the page, and what looked wrong. Your teacher can pass it on. Please do not post your name, school or email anywhere online to reach us.

Teachers and parents

Open an issue on the project's GitHub page. Describe the page and the problem; a screenshot helps. You need a free GitHub account.

Report on GitHub ↗

This site has no contact form: it would have to collect your details, and we keep personal information out of Junior Coders (Amānah).
