What is a Link?

A link is like a secret door that takes you to another page. We use the <a> tag (Anchor) to make them.

<a href="https://google.com">Click Me!</a>

The "href" Power-up

The href attribute tells the link where to go. Without it, the door doesn't lead anywhere!

Pro Tip: Use target="_blank" to open the link in a new tab so you don't lose your current page.
