What is Semantic HTML?

Semantic HTML means using tags that describe their **meaning**. Instead of just using a generic <div> for everything, we use specific tags!

  • <header>: For the top part of your site.
  • <nav>: For your navigation links.
  • <main>: For the most important content.
  • <footer>: For the bottom part.

Why is it smart?

It helps Google understand your site better (SEO) and it helps Screen Readers describe the page to people who are blind. It's the mark of a pro coder!
