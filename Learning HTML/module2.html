What are Attributes?

Attributes are like settings for your tags. They always live inside the opening tag. For example, if you have a tag for a link, the attribute tells the computer where the link goes!

How to write them

We write attributes like this: name="value". Look at this example:

<p class="super-text" lang="en">Hello!</p>

Here, class and lang are attributes. They help us group things or tell the computer what language we are using.
