Text & Headings

Giving your words a voice and structure.

Learning Objectives

In this module, you will master:

  • Using heading levels correctly (h1 to h6)
  • Creating clean paragraphs with <p>
  • Formatting text with bold, italics, and highlights
  • Using line breaks and dividers

The Hierarchy of Headings

Think of headings like a newspaper. The main story is the h1, sections are h2, and smaller details are h3.

<h1>The Big Title</h1>
<h2>A Major Section</h2>
<h3>A Smaller Subsection</h3>
Pro Tip: Never skip a level! Don't go from h1 straight to h3. It confuses search engines and screen readers.

Adding Emphasis

Sometimes you want to make a point. Use these tags to style your text:

  • 🔥 <strong>: Makes text bold
  • ✨ <em>: Makes text italic
  • 🖍️ <mark>: Highlights text
