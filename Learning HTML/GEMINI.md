# HTML Learning Context & Standards

The single source of truth for all HTML concepts, terminology, exercises, and challenges is the official textbook in `book-html/` (**HTML for Class 6 & 7: Learn to Structure the Web**).

---

## 1. Ethical Alignment (*'Ilm Nāfi'* & *Amānah*)

All HTML learning materials, code examples, form templates, and layout projects must strictly adhere to Islamic values:
- **Beneficial Knowledge & Wholesome Design (*'Ilm Nāfi'* & *Ḥayā'*)**: Frame webpage creation as building digital spaces that serve the community, honor family values, educate the youth, and facilitate ethical trade.
- **Strictly Prohibited Domains**:
  - **No Commercial Movie / Entertainment Streaming**: Do not build mockups or forms for movie streaming platforms (e.g., Netflix), cinema box offices, celebrity gossip fansites, or unwholesome media portals.
  - **No Usury / Conventional Banking (*Ribā*)**: No loan applications or interest calculators.
  - **No Gambling or Lotteries (*Maysir*)**: No lottery forms, raffle betting, or chance games.
  - **No Prohibited Goods (*Ḥarām*)**: No references to alcohol, nightclubs, or unwholesome venues.
- **Encouraged Wholesome Scenarios**:
  - Educational academy portals, digital libraries, school course catalogs, and student profiles.
  - Ethical commerce: Halal bakeries, bookstores, ethical clothing, honey and organic farms.
  - Science discovery exhibitions, planetarium visitor passes, botanical gardens, and community event registrations.
  - Charitable donation pages, orphan sponsorship cards, and disaster relief campaigns.
- **Digital Trust (*Amānah*)**: Emphasize proper form labeling, accessible navigation, honest content presentation without deceptive hidden text, and privacy-respecting form fields.

## 2. Curriculum & Module Mapping
All learning modules and practice pages must maintain direct alignment with the 17 chapters across the 8 textbook parts:
Module pages as they are on the site (`Learning HTML/moduleN.html`), with the textbook chapters each one covers:
- **Module 1**: Welcome to HTML & Page Anatomy (Book Part 1: Ch 1–3)
- **Module 2**: Tags & Attribute Superpowers (Book Part 2: Ch 4)
- **Module 3**: Headings & Paragraphs (Book Part 2: Ch 5)
- **Module 4**: Text Formatting (Book Part 2: Ch 6)
- **Module 5**: Links & Navigation (Book Part 3: Ch 7)
- **Module 6**: Images & Media (Book Part 3: Ch 8)
- **Module 7**: Tables & Data Grids (Book Part 4: Ch 10)
- **Module 8**: Lists & Grouping (Book Part 4: Ch 9)
- **Module 9**: Block vs Inline (Book Part 6: Ch 12)
- **Module 10**: Classes & IDs (Book Part 6: Ch 12)
- **Module 11**: Forms & User Interaction (Book Part 5: Ch 11)
- **Module 12**: Semantic HTML5 & Modern Layout (Book Part 6: Ch 12)
- **Module 13 / Lab Projects**: Real-World Website Projects & Capstone (Book Part 7 & 8: Ch 13–17)

**The page order above is the official module order (decided 2026-10-11).** It differs from the textbook in two places
(Tables before Lists; Forms after Block/Inline and Classes & IDs); that is intended. The textbook keeps its own chapter
order. Keep module pages free of concepts not yet taught in *page* order.

## 3. Educational Priorities
When developing or modifying pages in this directory:
1. **Source of Truth**: Draw code examples, terminology, and challenges directly from `book-html/`.
2. **Pedagogical Structure**: Every module must contain:
   - Clear learning goals.
   - Interactive live code editor with instant preview.
   - 3-level exercises directly from the book: 🟢 Easy (Recall), 🟡 Medium (Application), 🔴 Challenge (Creative Problem-Solving).
   - Instant-feedback interactive quiz.
   - Direct link to corresponding textbook chapter (`../book-html/#ch*`).
3. **Semantic HTML5 & Accessibility**: Enforce proper elements (`<header>`, `<nav>`, `<main>`, `<article>`, `<section>`, `<footer>`), valid `alt` text for images, and ARIA attributes where needed.
4. **Theme Consistency**: Inherit centralized platform styling from `../styles/main.css`.
