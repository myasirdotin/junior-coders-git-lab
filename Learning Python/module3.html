Two Types of Loops

for Loop

Use when you know in advance how many times to repeat (or iterating through a collection).

for item in items:

while Loop

Use when repeating until an unknown future event occurs (repeats while condition is True).

while battery > 10:

🧪 Your Turn: Daily Water Consumption Tracker

Calculate the total water collected across 5 rainfall events:

rain_readings = [12.5, 0.0, 18.2, 5.4, 22.0]
total_water = 0

for reading in rain_readings:
    total_water += reading
    print(f"Recorded +{reading} mm -> Total: {total_water:.1f} mm")

print(f"Summary: Harvested {total_water} mm of clean rain!")
