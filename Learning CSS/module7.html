Rows and Columns

While Flexbox is like a line, **Grid** is like a piece of graph paper. You define exactly how many rows and columns you want.

display: grid;
grid-template-columns: repeat(3, 1fr);

The "Fraction" Unit (fr) 🍕

Instead of pixels, Grid uses fr. It stands for "Fraction". It tells the browser to share the space like a pizza!

grid-template-columns: 1fr 2fr 1fr;

The middle column will be TWICE as big as the others!
