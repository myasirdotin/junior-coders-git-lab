Controlling the Workers

The "Boss" (Container) can give three main commands:

1. Direction ➡️

Are we a row or a column?

flex-direction: row;

2. Justify ↔️

Space them out horizontally.

justify-content: center;

3. Align ↕️

Center them vertically.

align-items: center;

The Layout Boss Sandbox 🪁

Try changing justify-content properties (like space-between, space-around, or center) to tell the worker boxes where to align!
