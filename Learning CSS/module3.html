The Gift Box Analogy

Imagine you are sending a gift in a box. The Box Model works exactly like that!

MARGIN (Space outside)
BORDER (The Box)
PADDING (Bubble Wrap)
CONTENT (The Gift)

The "Bubble Wrap" Rule

1. Padding

Space inside the border. It protects the content.

padding: 20px;

2. Border

The edge of the box. Can be thick, thin, or dotted!

border: 5px solid red;

3. Margin

Space outside the border. It pushes other boxes away.

margin: 30px;
