Hygiene, Backups & Forensics 💻🧹

Everyday defensive discipline: defeat malware, apply the 3-2-1 backup rule, and master incident response.

The Malware Spectrum & Automatic Updates

Ransomware, trojans, worms, and spyware exploit known operating system bugs. Enable **automatic security updates** on Windows, macOS, and mobile devices to patch zero-day holes before attackers weaponize them.

The 3-2-1 Backup Rule: Ransomware Immunity

• 3 Copies of Data: Primary original + 2 independent backups.
• 2 Different Media: E.g. internal computer drive + external USB drive.
• 1 Offsite Copy: Encrypted cloud storage or remote drive at another home.

If ransomware strikes or your laptop is lost, a 3-2-1 backup allows full recovery without paying extortion!

Metadata Privacy Hygiene

Photos taken with smartphones store hidden EXIF metadata containing exact GPS latitude and longitude coordinates. Always strip EXIF data before uploading photos to public community portals.

Incident Response: The 6 NIST Phases

  1. Preparation: Audit logging, backups, and defined team roles.
  2. Identification: Detecting anomalies and alert triage.
  3. Containment: Isolate the compromised host from the network. (Do NOT pull power cord—save volatile RAM memory!).
  4. Eradication: Remove malware, close backdoors, and rotate all keys.
  5. Recovery: Restore clean systems from verified backups under close observation.
  6. Lessons Learned: Blameless post-mortem analysis to harden future defenses.
