Phishing Detective & Email Header Inspector

From: "Junior Coders Academy" <security-alerts@juni0r-coders.net>
To: student@school.edu
Subject: URGENT: Verify your scholarship account within 6 hours or lose access!
SPF: SoftFail DKIM: None

Dear Student,

We detected unauthorized login attempts to your academic portfolio from an unrecognized device in Eastern Europe.

To avoid immediate account termination and maintain your scholarship status, please verify your credentials below:

🔒 Verify Account Credentials Immediately
Hover preview: http://198.51.100.88/fake-login/harvest.php

If you fail to complete verification before 18:00 today, your access will be permanently revoked.

Sincerely,
Global Support Desk

🚩 Forensic Deception Analysis:

  • Lookalike Typosquatting: juni0r-coders.net uses a zero 0 instead of the letter o!
  • Coercive Urgency: Demands action in "6 hours" to induce panic and disable critical thinking.
  • Mismatched IP Destination: The button links directly to an unencrypted HTTP raw IP address running a credential harvester.
  • Failed Authentication: SPF SoftFail indicates the email was dispatched from an unauthorized mail server.
