Hacking the Human Mind

Attackers do not break algorithms when they can simply trick a person into opening the door. They weaponize psychological triggers:

Artificial Urgency ⏱️

"Act in 20 minutes or your account will be deleted permanently!" Panic bypasses logic.

Fake Authority 👔

Impersonating bank directors, school principals, or police officers to force quick compliance.

Curiosity & Baiting 🎁

"Click here to see who viewed your profile" or infected flash drives left in hallways.

Helpfulness Exploit 🤝

Posing as a distressed new student who "forgot their login password" to gain staff empathy.

Spotting Phishing Emails & Fraud

🚩 Lookalike Domain: support@g00gle.com or paypaI.com (Capital I instead of l)
🚩 Mismatched Links: Text displays https://mybank.com but points to http://198.51.100.88/scam
🚩 Unsolicited MFA Requests: "Read me the SMS code we just sent your phone"
🚩 Coercive Threats: Warnings of sudden legal action or scholarship loss

The Principle of Verification (*Tabayyun*)

Whenever you receive an urgent message requesting credentials, money, or sensitive files, execute the **Out-of-Band Verification**: Hang up, navigate to the organization's official website manually in your browser, and call their public phone number directly.
