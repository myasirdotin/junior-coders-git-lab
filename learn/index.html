Our Educational Philosophy: Technology as a Sacred Trust (Amānah)

At Junior Coders, programming is not just syntax—it is a tool for beneficial knowledge ('Ilm Nāfi'). We teach students to write honest code, protect user privacy, reject deceptive patterns, and practice excellence (Iḥsān) in every line they craft.
