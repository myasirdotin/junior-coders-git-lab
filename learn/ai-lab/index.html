AI Lab & Ethical AI Literacy 🤖✨

Demystifying artificial intelligence for the next generation of builders. Learn how large language models predict tokens, master precision prompt engineering, detect hallucinations with rigorous fact-checking, and uphold ethical data stewardship (*Amānah*).

📋 Prerequisites

  • Critical thinking and reading comprehension skills.
  • Basic familiarity with computer web browsers and search.
  • Python basics recommended for the API module, but not required for prompt literacy.
  • A deep respect for truthfulness (*Ṣidq*) and verification (*Tabayyun*).

🎯 What You Will Learn

  • The true mental model of AI: probabilistic token prediction, not conscious minds.
  • Precision Prompt Engineering: Role, Task, Context, Format, and Constraints (RTCFC).
  • Understanding hallucinations: Why AI invents convincing facts and how to spot them.
  • Rigorous source verification and the Qur'anic principle of *Tabayyun* (investigating news).
  • Data privacy as a sacred trust (*Amānah*): preventing leaks of private photos and personal secrets.

🗺️ Clear Progression Map

A four-phase journey from passive AI consumer to discerning, ethical AI creator.

Step 01
How AI "Thinks"
Tokens, statistical prediction, training data, weights, and embeddings.
Step 02
Prompt Craftsmanship
Role prompting, chain-of-thought, few-shot examples, and output constraints.
Step 03
Hallucinations & Tabayyun
Red-teaming responses, detecting fabrications, and multi-source verification.
Step 04
Ethical Stewardship
Data privacy (*Amānah*), algorithmic bias, environmental energy costs, and beneficial use.

🔍

The Qur'anic Principle of Verification: *Tabayyun* (تبين)

"O you who have believed, if there comes to you a disobedient one with information, investigate (fatabayyanū), lest you harm a people out of ignorance..." (Surah Al-Hujurat 49:6). In an age where generative AI can synthesize realistic falsehoods in milliseconds, verifying information against original authoritative sources before believing or sharing it is a sacred moral duty.

📖 Cross-Reference Primary Sources 🚫 Never Trust AI Citations Blindly ⚖️ Academic Honesty (*Ṣidq*)
