Cybersecurity & Defensive Computing 🛡️🔐

In an interconnected world, defensive engineering is a fundamental moral and technical responsibility. Master the CIA Triad, password entropy mathematics, web defense (eliminating SQLi & XSS), phishing detection, and the sacred trust of user data (*Amānah*).

📋 Prerequisites

  • Basic knowledge of web browsers and computer networks.
  • Understanding of HTML forms and basic logic is helpful.
  • Commitment to purely defensive, ethical technology (*Ḥimāyah*).
  • Zero tolerance for unauthorized testing, spying, or malicious attacks.

🎯 What You Will Learn

  • The CIA Triad (Confidentiality, Integrity, Availability) and threat modeling.
  • Password entropy mathematics ($E = L \times \log_2(N)$) and modern key derivation.
  • OWASP Top 10 web vulnerabilities: preventing SQL Injection and Cross-Site Scripting.
  • HTTP defensive headers (HSTS, CSP) and session cookie fortification (`HttpOnly`, `Secure`).
  • Personal digital hygiene: multi-factor authentication (MFA) and the 3-2-1 backup rule.

🗺️ Clear Progression Map

A four-tier defensive ascension from security basics to enterprise defense-in-depth.

Step 01
Threat Landscape
CIA Triad, attack surfaces, adversaries, and defense-in-depth principles.
Step 02
Cryptographic Defense
Hashing vs encryption vs encoding, salts, and memory-hard Argon2id.
Step 03
OWASP Web Defense
Prepared statements (SQLi fix), output encoding (XSS fix), and anti-CSRF tokens.
Step 04
Hygiene & Resiliency
Phishing detective, 3-2-1 backup rule, ransomware resilience, and incident response.
