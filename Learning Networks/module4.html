Transport Layer: TCP vs. UDP 🚚⚡

Guaranteed reliability vs. lightning-fast speed. Understand handshakes, sockets, and ports.

The TCP 3-Way Handshake

Before TCP transfers a single byte of data, it establishes an synchronized connection between client and server:

1. Client ➔ Server: [SYN] (Seq=1000) "Let's connect! My sequence starts at 1000."
2. Server ➔ Client: [SYN-ACK] (Seq=5000, Ack=1001) "Got it! My sequence is 5000."
3. Client ➔ Server: [ACK] (Seq=1001, Ack=5001) "Acknowledged! Connection ESTABLISHED."

TCP vs. UDP Breakdown

TCP (Transmission Control Protocol)

Guarantees delivery, preserves packet order, and handles retransmission.

Best for: Web pages (HTTP/HTTPS), email, banking, and file downloads.

UDP (User Datagram Protocol)

Connectionless, ultra-low 8-byte header, zero handshake latency.

Best for: Live streaming, online multiplayer games, VoIP calls, and DNS queries.
