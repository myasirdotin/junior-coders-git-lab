Our Ethical Foundation: Technology as a Sacred Trust (Amānah)

At Junior Coders, we believe engineering skills must be paired with moral integrity. Code is an instrument for beneficial knowledge ('Ilm Nāfi') that solves genuine human problems, protects families and communities, respects user data as an inviolable trust (Amānah), and reflects craftsmanship and thoroughness (Iḥsān).
